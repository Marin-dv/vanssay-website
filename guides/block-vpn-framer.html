How to block VPN & proxy traffic on a Framer site

Any country block can be stepped around by a $3/month VPN, because geolocation only sees the IP address. If your restriction actually matters, licensing, gated launches, fraud, compliance, you need VPN detection on top. Here's how to add it to a Framer site.

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

Why country blocking alone isn't enough

Geoblocking maps the visitor's IP to a country. A VPN gives the visitor a different IP in a country of their choosing, so to your block rule, a user in a blocked region connecting through a US VPN server is a US visitor. If you're blocking countries for anything with stakes (regulated products, region-locked content, contest eligibility, scraper defense), the VPN route is the first thing people try.

How VPN detection actually works

You can't ask a browser "are you on a VPN?", detection happens server-side by classifying the IP itself:

  • Datacenter ranges: VPN servers live in datacenters (AWS, M247, Datacamp…). Real visitors come from residential and mobile ISPs. An IP announced by a hosting provider is almost never a human on their home connection.
  • Known exit nodes: commercial VPN providers' server IPs are catalogued and continuously refreshed in IP-intelligence databases.
  • Proxy & Tor lists: open proxies and Tor relays are public and easy to match.

Honest caveat: no detection is 100%. Fresh exit nodes and residential-proxy services can slip through, and rare false positives exist (e.g. some corporate networks). In practice, IP-intelligence blocking stops the mainstream VPN apps, NordVPN, Surfshark, Proton, Mullvad and the like, which is where nearly all bypass traffic comes from.

FAQ

Will it block normal visitors by mistake?

False positives are rare and mostly limited to traffic that already looks non-residential (corporate VPNs, some hosting-based networks). Regular home and mobile connections pass untouched.

Can I block VPNs without blocking any country?

Yes, VPN/proxy detection is an independent rule. You can allow every country and still turn away anonymized traffic.

Does it slow my site down?

The check is a single fast server-side lookup at load time; visitors don't notice it, and your Framer pages are untouched otherwise.
