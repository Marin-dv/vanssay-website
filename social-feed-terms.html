Social Feed: Terms and Conditions

For the Webflow App "Social Feed - Vanssay". Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version. Social Feed shows the Instagram posts of an account you own or are authorized to represent on your Webflow site. You need a free Vanssay account to place a feed. 2. What the App does

  • You enter the handle of a public Instagram account you own or are authorized to represent. We retrieve that account's public profile and recent posts and show you a preview in seven layouts (Profile, Grid, Slider, Aperture, Carousel, Marquee and Rainbow).
  • When you insert a feed, the App places a labelled placeholder element on your Designer canvas. The Designer canvas does not run scripts, so the posts themselves are not shown there.
  • The App adds one script to your site's footer (see §6). On your published site, that script finds the placeholder and shows the feed in its place.
  • Feeds are not real-time. They show the posts we last retrieved. How often that happens depends on your plan (see §4).

5. Instagram content and your rights

  • Only accounts you own or represent. You may connect only an Instagram account that you own or are authorized to represent. A profile being public does not give you that right.
  • Your rights to the content. You confirm that you have the rights needed for that account's posts, captions, images and videos to be retrieved, stored and displayed through the App on your site, and that your use complies with Instagram's and Meta's terms. You are responsible for that content on your site.
  • How we retrieve it. We retrieve the public profile and posts of the account you connect through a third-party data provider (Apify). We never ask for an Instagram password and never log in to Instagram as you.
  • We store copies of the media. Instagram's image and video links expire after a few days. So for every feed you save, we download that feed's pictures and videos, store them on our servers and serve them to your visitors from there. By saving a feed you give us permission to do this for as long as the feed exists. Previewing a profile stores no media.
  • Cached copies. Posts are cached on our servers, so a feed may keep showing a post for a while after it is deleted on Instagram, until the next refresh. A Free feed keeps showing it until you refresh it by hand.
  • Deleting a feed deletes its media from our servers, unless another account still has a feed for the same Instagram profile. To have content removed from our cache at any time, email support@vanssay.net.
  • Takedown requests. If you believe a feed shows content you own without permission, email us with the details. We will review it and remove the content where appropriate.

6. The code we add to your Webflow site

  • What we add. One script, registered with Webflow under the name VanssayInstagram and applied to your site-wide footer custom code. It is loaded from api.vanssay.net, a fixed version with an integrity hash, so it cannot change once registered. It shows your feeds where you placed them and does nothing else on your pages. What it sends to our server is described in the Privacy Policy, §4.
  • How we add it. Only through Webflow's official Custom Code API, only after you authorize the App for that site, and only when you insert a feed. Your other custom code is left as it is. We never ask you to paste code by hand.
  • Updates. A new version of the script is registered as a new version and applied to your site only when you next insert a feed. We never change a registered version in place.
  • Removing it. Use Remove from this site in the App's dashboard. This removes every version of our script from your site and leaves your other custom code untouched. Then publish your site. Two things stay in your Designer project for you to delete by hand, because only you can edit your canvas: the placeholder element of each feed, and the styles named "Vanssay Social Feed (placeholder…)". Step-by-step instructions are in the documentation.
  • Uninstalling. Remove the script first, then uninstall the App in Webflow. Uninstalling withdraws our access to your site.
  • Your authorization. Authorize only sites you own or are authorized to manage. The access token Webflow issues is stored encrypted and used only for the purposes above.
  • Custom code and your Webflow plan. Whether Webflow runs custom code on your published site depends on your Webflow site and Workspace plan. That is set by Webflow, not by us.
