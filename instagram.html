FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to connect my Instagram account?

No. You paste a public handle and that's it — no Instagram login, no Meta developer app, no access token to renew every 60 days. It works for your own profile or for a client's, and you never hand us a password.

Does it work outside Framer?

Yes. Open the web app at instagram.vanssay.net, sign in with the same account, and copy one snippet into Webflow, WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify, Wix or any site that accepts HTML. Style changes you make there reach every page with the code within a few minutes.

On Free, the feed only shows on .framer.website and .webflow.io addresses. Any other site needs a paid plan. On Framer, the plugin is still the easiest route: it inserts a native component you style from Framer's own panel.

Does the snippet work on every builder's free plan?

Not always. Some builders only run custom code on their own paid plans, like Webflow's Code Embed. That's the builder's rule, not ours: on any page that accepts HTML, the snippet works.

Does the feed update by itself?

Yes. The backend refreshes each connected feed on a schedule (daily on Starter and Pro, weekly on Lifetime) and your published site reads the refreshed copy. You can also hit "Sync now" in the plugin when you've just posted something.

What is a "feed view"?

One load of your widget by a visitor on your published site. Starter includes 10,000 a month and Pro 50,000 — well past what a typical marketing site does. You'll get an email before it ever becomes a problem.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes — $0 forever, no card required. It's capped to 1 feed, 6 posts and a manual once-a-day refresh, since automatic syncing is what actually costs us money at our scraping provider. Upgrade to Starter or Pro when you want the feed to sync itself.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep the plugin until the end of the period you paid for, then the feed stops refreshing and the widget stops loading new posts. Cancel in two clicks from your account page — it opens Stripe's own billing portal, no email required.

Can I choose exactly which posts show?

Yes. Switch a feed to Curated and hand-pick from a pool of your 50 most recent posts. The picked set stays fixed until you change it, which is what you want for a portfolio or a lookbook.

Will it slow my site down?

No. The widget fetches one small JSON payload from our cache and images are proxied and cached on our side, so Instagram's signed URLs can't expire mid-life on your page.
