Social Feed documentation

How to install, use and remove the Webflow App "Social Feed - Vanssay". Last updated: October 17, 2026

What it does. Social Feed shows the posts of an Instagram account you own or manage on your Webflow site. You connect the account, pick one of seven layouts, and place the feed on your page from the Designer. On your published site, one script added to your site's footer shows the feed in that spot. A free Vanssay account is required to place a feed.

1. Installing the App

  1. Install Social Feed - Vanssay from the Webflow App Marketplace and choose the site to use it on.
  2. Open that site in the Webflow Designer.
  3. Open the Apps panel in the left toolbar and launch Social Feed - Vanssay. It opens as a panel.

No Instagram login. The App reads a public Instagram profile by its handle. You never connect your Instagram login and never enter an Instagram password.

2. Connecting an Instagram account

  1. Type the handle of an Instagram account you own or are authorized to represent, with or without the @, or paste its instagram.com link.
  2. Press Preview Feed. The profile and its recent posts load so you can see the feed before placing it. You do not need an account to preview.

The account must be public. You must also have the rights for its posts to be shown on your site. A profile being public does not give you those rights: see the Terms, §5.

3. Layouts

There are seven layouts. Click any of them in the panel to see your posts in it.

LayoutWhat it looks like
ProfileA profile header (picture, name, counts) above a grid of posts.
GridA square gallery in the number of columns you choose.
SliderA row of posts visitors swipe or drag through.
ApertureVertical slices that open up as the visitor moves across them.
CarouselA coverflow where the side posts step back for depth.

Clicking a post on your site opens it in a popup with its caption, date and, for videos, playback.

4. Settings

You choose the layout in the App. The App places the feed with sensible defaults (3 columns, 4px gap, 8px corners, header on, captions off). To change them, select the feed's placeholder on the canvas, open the Settings panel (gear icon) and edit its custom attributes, then publish:

AttributeWhat it does
data-columnsHow many posts sit side by side (1 to 6).
data-gapSpace between posts, in pixels.
data-radiusHow rounded each post is, in pixels.
data-limitHow many posts the feed shows, up to your plan's limit.
data-header1 shows the profile picture, name and counts above the feed, 0 hides them.
data-captions1 shows each post's caption, 0 hides it.
data-apertureAperture only: the shape of the opened slice (1 square, 0.8 portrait, 1.25 landscape).

Leave data-vanssay-instagram and data-token as they are: they tell the script which feed to show.

By default a feed shows the account's latest posts. On a paid plan you can switch to Curated and hand-pick which posts appear, from the latest 50.

5. Placing the feed and publishing

  1. Sign in, or create a free Vanssay account (email and password), when the App asks. This is needed to save a feed.
  2. On the canvas, select the section or element where the feed should go.
  3. In the App, press Insert to Canvas.
  4. The first time on a site, Webflow asks you to authorize the App for that site. This lets the App add its script to your site's footer (see §6). If you decline, nothing is placed and nothing is changed.
  5. A labelled placeholder appears on the canvas, showing the feed's layout and handle. This is expected: the Designer canvas does not run scripts, so the posts do not show there.
  6. Publish your site. The feed appears on the published site in place of the placeholder.

To see the feed in Webflow's Preview, turn on Site settings → Custom code → Run custom code in Preview. This is a one-time setting per site.

You can move, resize or style the placeholder's container like any element. To change the display settings later, edit the placeholder's attributes (see §4), then publish your site.

6. What the App adds to your site

  • What: one script, shown in Webflow as VanssayInstagram. It is loaded from api.vanssay.net as a fixed version with an integrity hash, so its content cannot change once registered.
  • Where: your site's footer custom code, site-wide, so a feed works on any page you place it on. It is added through Webflow's Custom Code API. Your other custom code is not changed.
  • What it does: it finds the feed placeholders on the page and shows the feed in their place. On pages without a feed, it does nothing.
  • External resources: the script, the feed's posts and its images and videos all come from api.vanssay.net. Nothing is loaded from Instagram or any other server.
  • Data: each page load with a feed adds one to that feed's monthly view count and records the site address. No cookies, no browser storage, nothing stored about the visitor. Details are in the Privacy Policy, §4.

Webflow runs site custom code only on plans that include it. If the feed does not appear after publishing, check that your Webflow site or Workspace plan includes custom code.

7. How and when the feed refreshes

A feed is not real-time. It shows the posts we last retrieved from Instagram, and how often that happens depends on your plan:

  • Free: never on its own. The feed shows the posts from your last refresh until you press Sync now in the App (once a day).
  • Starter and Pro: automatically about once a day, plus manual refreshes from the App (3 or 10 a day).
  • Lifetime: automatically about once a week, plus 1 manual refresh a day.

A new Instagram post can therefore take up to a day (Starter, Pro) or a week (Lifetime) to appear, or until you refresh by hand. A Curated feed shows only the posts you picked and does not add new ones. You do not need to republish your site for a refresh to show.

9. Your account and billing

You create your Vanssay account inside the App. The same account works across Vanssay products. To change plan, update your card, get invoices or cancel, open your account page (also reachable from the App's Account tab). Cancelling stops future charges, and your plan stays active until the end of the period already paid.

10. Removing the feed and the App

Remove one feed from a page

  1. On the canvas, select the feed's placeholder element and delete it.
  2. Publish your site.

Remove the App's script from a site

  1. Open the App in the Designer and go to the Account tab, section Installed code.
  2. Press Remove from this site and confirm. Every version of the App's script is removed from your site's custom code. Your other custom code stays as it is.
  3. Delete the feed placeholders still on your pages. In the Style panel, delete the styles named Vanssay Social Feed (placeholder…). The App cannot delete canvas elements or styles for you.
  4. Publish your site.

Uninstall the App

  1. Remove the script first, as above. Uninstalling withdraws the App's access to your site, after which it can no longer remove its script for you.
  2. In Webflow, open your Workspace or site settings, go to Apps & Integrations, find Social Feed - Vanssay and uninstall it.
  3. If you uninstalled before removing the script, open Site settings → Custom code and delete the VanssayInstagram script there, then publish.

Your Vanssay account and saved feeds are kept after uninstalling, so you can come back. To delete them, delete the feeds in the App, or email support@vanssay.net to delete your account.

11. Troubleshooting

The canvas shows a placeholder, not my posts

That is expected. The Designer canvas does not run scripts. Publish the site, or turn on "Run custom code in Preview" to see it in Preview.

The feed shows nothing on my published site

  • Check that you published after inserting the feed.
  • On the Free plan, check that you are looking at your *.webflow.io address. On a custom domain, a Free feed shows nothing.
  • Check that your Webflow plan runs custom code (see §6).
  • Check that the Instagram account is still public.

A new post is not showing

Feeds refresh on your plan's schedule, not instantly (see §7). Press Sync now in the App to update it now. A Curated feed only shows the posts you picked.
