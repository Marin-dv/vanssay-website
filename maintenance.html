FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I still see my site during maintenance?

Add your IP to the whitelist (one click grabs your current IP). Whitelisted visitors bypass the maintenance page and load the real site, so you can keep working while everyone else sees "back soon".

Do I have to republish every time I toggle it?

No. You install the script once (one publish). After that, turning maintenance on/off, editing the page, changing the whitelist or the redirect all take effect live: the decision is made server-side on each visit.

What if my IP changes and I get locked out?

Add a CIDR range (e.g. your office network) instead of a single IP, or just re-open the plugin and re-add your current IP. The whitelist updates instantly.

Will it break my published site?

No. It only adds a fixed overlay when maintenance is on and fails open if the backend is ever unreachable, so visitors are never stuck on a broken page.

Does one plan cover multiple sites?

Yes, your plan is tied to your Vanssay account, not a single project.
