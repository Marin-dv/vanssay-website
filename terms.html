1. The Service & our plugins

Vanssay provides Framer plugins that share one Vanssay account. Currently:

  • Google & Trustpilot Reviews, displays reviews from your own Google Business Profile and/or Trustpilot page as widgets on your Framer site.
  • Instagram Feed, displays the posts of a public Instagram account you own or represent as widgets on your Framer site.
  • Bouncer, controls access to your Framer site by visitor country and VPN/proxy status.
  • Router, redirects or suggests pages to visitors based on country, region, language, device or VPN/proxy status, with optional UTM/query-string preservation and a soft suggestion banner.
  • Maintenance Mode, puts your site behind a maintenance page with an IP whitelist so you retain access (availability may vary; some plugins are released progressively).

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, Trustpilot, Instagram, Meta, Framer or Stripe.
