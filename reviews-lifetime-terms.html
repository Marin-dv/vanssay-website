1. What "Lifetime" means

"Lifetime" means the operating life of the Reviews service, not your own lifetime and not an unlimited period of time. It is a licence to use the plugin and its backend for as long as we operate that service, subject to the guaranteed minimum in section 3.

It is not a perpetual right, not a transfer of ownership, and not a promise that the service will exist forever. Software that depends on other companies' platforms cannot honestly promise that, and we would rather say so here than imply otherwise.

2. What is included

A Lifetime purchase includes the Reviews plugin at the tier described on the plan screen at the time you bought it. As of the current version that is:

  • 100,000 widget views / month
  • Up to 2 connected businesses
  • All layouts, including any added later
  • Up to 20 Google reviews and 20 Trustpilot reviews per business, and the combined cross-platform rating badge
  • Weekly automatic sync
  • Curate — pin and hide individual reviews
  • SEO stars in Google search results, and the "Powered by Vanssay" watermark removed
  • Insertion into the Framer canvas, use on your own domains, and priority support

It also includes any new layouts and improvements we add to this plugin, at no extra cost.

It does not include:

  • Our other plugins and products, current or future, including any successor product sold under a different name
  • Real-time sync — Lifetime refreshes weekly, not on every visit. That difference is what makes a single payment sustainable: a plan that re-fetched continuously for as long as the guarantee in section 3 runs would cost more than it was paid for
  • Features that require a materially higher ongoing cost to run, which we may offer as paid add-ons
  • Framer's own subscription, which you buy from Framer
  • Custom development, migration work, or support beyond ordinary product support

3. How long it is guaranteed

We commit to operating the Reviews service for at least 10 years from the date of your purchase. For a purchase made in 2026, that means until at least 2036.

During that period we will keep the service running, keep your reviews syncing, and keep your widgets served, subject to the rest of this page.

After the guaranteed period ends, we may discontinue the service. If we do:

  • We will give you at least six months' written notice, sent to the email address on your Vanssay account. It is your responsibility to keep that address current and able to receive our email.
  • The service will keep working normally throughout the notice period, so you have time to replace it on any live site.
  • At the end of the notice period the widget stops being served. Widgets already placed on your pages will simply stop showing content. Your Framer site is not otherwise affected.
  • No refund is due at that point, because the guaranteed period has been delivered in full.

If we discontinue the service before the guaranteed period ends for any reason other than those in section 4 or section 10, you may ask for a refund of the purchase price, reduced in proportion to the time already served.

Reviews works by retrieving your businesses' reviews and ratings from Trustpilot and Google, through their respective official APIs (the Trustpilot Business API and the Google Places API). Trustpilot and Google are not our partners and do not endorse, support or sponsor this product. The plugin also depends on Framer, on Stripe for payment, and on our hosting provider.

You accept that:

  • Trustpilot or Google may change, restrict, rate-limit, reprice, or revoke API access at any time, without notice to us, and may do so permanently.
  • The business listing you connect may be delisted, merged, suspended, or become otherwise inaccessible on Trustpilot or Google, or its identifier (place ID, business unit) may change. Any of these stops the widget syncing.
  • Individual reviews may be edited, hidden or removed by their author or by the platform at any time.
  • Google's public API tier currently returns a small, fixed number of reviews per business — a limit Google sets, not us. If Google changes what that tier returns, the widget's Google side changes with it.
  • Our providers may change their pricing, their terms, or their ability to serve this data, and we may have to switch provider, change how often businesses sync, or reduce what a widget can retrieve.
  • Framer may change its plugin platform in ways that require changes to, or removal of, features.

None of these is a defect in the Service and none of them gives rise to a refund. If access to Trustpilot or Google review data becomes permanently impossible, or possible only at a cost that makes the product unviable, we may discontinue the service before the guaranteed period in section 3. In that case we will give you as much notice as the circumstances allow, and we will tell you honestly what happened.

9. Your reviews and our copies

The reviews displayed by your widget were written by your customers and belong to them and to the platform they were posted on (Trustpilot or Google). You confirm you own or represent the business whose reviews are displayed and that you have the right to show them on your site.

To serve your widget reliably and to build the cross-platform rating badge, we cache review content and ratings on our servers between syncs. A widget also carries a copy of the reviews that were present when you inserted it, which means a widget can briefly show a review after it has been edited or removed on the source platform.

If you need cached content removed, write to support@vanssay.net and we will remove it. Removing a business stops further syncing and removes it from our systems, but it does not alter pages you have already published; you also need to remove the widget in Framer and republish.
