FAQ

Questions, answered

Which platforms does it work on today?

Framer, through the Framer Marketplace. Versions for Webflow, Wix and Shopify are in construction and run on the same engine. Ask to be notified when yours ships.

Where do the reviews come from?

Your public Google and Trustpilot pages. You search your business by name, we find it on both and combine the ratings into one score. No API keys needed.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, a free plan you can keep, not a trial. Paid plans add more layouts, reviews and views. See pricing.

Do reviews update on their own?

Yes. We sync your reviews every day on our servers, and the widget on your published site picks them up without a republish.
