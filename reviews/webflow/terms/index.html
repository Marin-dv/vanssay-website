2. What the App does

  • You connect a Google Business Profile you own or manage (by signing in with Google), or search a business (up to 5 public reviews). We import its reviews and you choose which show, in one of eight layouts (Grid, Slider, Masonry, Marquee, List, Rating card, Badge and Floating).
  • With the Designer app, you place a widget block on a page. The Designer canvas does not run custom code, so the block shows a placeholder there.
  • The App adds one script to your site's footer (see §6). On your published site, that script shows the widget in place of the block.
  • Widgets are not real-time. They show the reviews we last retrieved from Google, refreshed about once every 24 hours.

5. Google reviews and your rights

  • Only businesses you own or manage. When you sign in with Google, you may connect only Business Profiles your Google account is an owner or manager of. With the search, you may show the reviews of your own business or one you are authorized to represent.
  • Reviews are not ours or yours. Reviews belong to their authors and are provided by Google under Google's terms. The App shows them as Google provides them, with the reviewer's name, rating, date and text, and a link to write a review on Google. It does not let you edit a review's content. You are responsible for how you present reviews on your site, including any rules that apply to you on the presentation of customer reviews.
  • Read only. The App never replies to reviews or changes anything on your Business Profile (see the Privacy Policy, §3).
  • Cached copies. Reviews are stored on our servers so widgets load quickly. A review deleted or edited on Google can keep showing until the next refresh, about a day later.
  • Takedown requests. If you believe a widget shows content in breach of your rights, email us with the details. We will review it and act where appropriate. Reviews themselves are managed on Google.

6. The code we add to your Webflow site

  • What we add. One script, registered with Webflow under the name VanssayReviews and applied to your site-wide footer custom code. It is loaded from api.vanssay.net, a fixed version with an integrity hash, so it cannot change once registered. It shows your widgets where you placed them and does nothing else on your pages. What it sends is described in the Privacy Policy, §5.
  • How we add it. Only through Webflow's official Custom Code API, only on the sites you authorize when you install the App, or when you add a site or turn the App back on. Your other custom code is left as it is. We never ask you to paste code by hand, and the App never publishes your site for you.
  • Updates. A new version of the script is registered as a new version. We never change a registered version in place.
  • Removing it. Use Remove from this site in the web app or the Designer app. This removes every version of our script from that site and leaves your other custom code untouched. Then publish your site.
  • Uninstalling. Uninstalling the App in Webflow ends our access, and Webflow removes the script the App added (live after your next publish). Two things stay in your Designer project for you to delete, because only you can edit your canvas: the widget blocks you placed, and the style named "Vanssay Reviews placeholder". Step-by-step instructions are in the documentation.
  • Your authorization. Authorize only sites you own or are authorized to manage. The access token Webflow issues is stored encrypted and used only for the purposes above.
  • Custom code and your Webflow plan. Whether Webflow runs custom code on your published site depends on your Webflow site and Workspace plan. That is set by Webflow, not by us.
