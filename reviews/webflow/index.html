What it does

All your reviews, not just five.

Google's public search only shares five reviews per business. Signed in as the owner or a manager of the Business Profile, the app gets all of them.

  • Every review, with its photos and your reply
  • 8 layouts: grid, slider, masonry, marquee, list, rating card, badge and a floating card
  • Pick, hide or pin reviews, filter by stars or words
  • Synced every day, nothing to update by hand
  • Free on your .webflow.io address. Custom domains with Starter ($7.99/month) or Pro ($17.99/month, up to 10 businesses), 7-day free trial
  • No Google sign-in? Search your business and show up to five public reviews.

Your Google data

We read your reviews. We never change your profile.

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to your Business Profile. It uses that access to read two things: the list of businesses you manage, so you can pick one, and the reviews of the businesses you pick (rating, text, date, the reviewer's public name and photo, review photos and your replies).

  • Read only. We never reply to reviews, edit your listing, post or change anything on your profile.
  • One use. The reviews are shown in your widgets, on the websites you choose, and in your dashboard. That's all.
  • Never shared or sold. No advertising, no data brokers, no AI training.
  • Yours to remove. Click Disconnect Google in the app and we revoke the access and delete the reviews we stored, straight away.

Full details in the Reviews - Vanssay Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
