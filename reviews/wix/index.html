Vanssay Reviews / Wix

Google Reviews 2027 for Wix

Show your Google reviews on your Wix site, with photos and your replies, always in sync. This page explains how to set it up and answers the common questions.

Set it up → Contact support

Setup

Four steps, about two minutes

The app's dashboard (Apps → Google Reviews in your Wix dashboard) shows the same steps and ticks them off as you go.

STEP 1

Add the app

Add Google Reviews 2027 from the Wix App Market. Its dashboard opens.

STEP 2

Connect Google

Click Sign in with Google with the Google account that manages your Business Profile, then pick the business. Every review comes in, with photos and your replies. No access to the profile? Use Search your business (Google then shares 5 reviews), or on a paid plan send a client link to the person who manages it.

STEP 3

Add the widget

In the Wix editor: Add Elements → App Widgets → Google Reviews 2027. Click the widget, then Settings to pick a layout and match your site's colors and fonts.

STEP 4

Publish

Publish your site. The dashboard then shows the pages where the widget is live and how many times it was viewed.

Customize

Choose what shows, and how.

In the app's dashboard, click Edit on a widget for a live preview with every setting, and a Reviews tab listing each review with the reason it shows or not.

  • 10 layouts: slider, floating badge, grid, masonry, list, marquee, rating card, badge, rating strip and floating card
  • Your colors, fonts, corners and wording
  • Minimum stars, hide single reviews, pin your favorites first
  • Exclude words or names, choose the order

Questions

Common questions

Why do I only see 5 reviews?

A business found with Search your business only gets the 5 reviews Google shares publicly. Sign in with Google (the account that manages the Business Profile) to show all of them.

How often do new reviews appear?

Automatically: once a week on the Free plan, every day on paid plans.

Can I hide a review?

Yes, on every plan: in the dashboard, Edit → Reviews → Hide. You can also set a minimum star rating.

I build sites for clients. Do I need their Google password?

No. On a paid plan, create a client link in the dashboard and send it. Your client signs in with Google on our page, and their reviews come to the site. They don't need Wix access.

What does Google access let you do?

We only read your reviews and replies. We never reply, edit your profile or post anything. Details in our privacy policy.

What happens if I remove the app?

The widgets stop showing, we revoke the Google access and delete your site's data (privacy policy §7b).

Something else?

Write to support@vanssay.net. We answer every email.

On Framer or Webflow too?

Vanssay Reviews runs on other site builders as well.
