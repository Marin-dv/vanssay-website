Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Vanssay collects, why, on what legal basis, and your rights. It applies to our website (vanssay.net) and our Framer plugins: Google & Trustpilot Reviews, Bouncer, Router and Maintenance Mode.

Data controller. Marin de Vanssay (sole trader), operating vanssay.net, is the controller of the account data described here. Contact for any privacy request: support@vanssay.net.
Two roles. For your account and billing, we are the controller. For the processing of your site visitors' data by Bouncer, Router or Maintenance Mode, you are the controller and we act as your processor (see §3).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). Optionally the plugin you signed up from.
  • Plugin configuration: your settings per plugin, for Reviews, the profiles/pages you connect plus widget settings; for Bouncer, your blocking rules and screen styling; for Router, your redirect/suggestion rules (countries, regions, languages, devices, VPN options, destinations, UTM settings); for Maintenance Mode, your whitelist, page content and schedule.
  • Review data: publicly available reviews fetched from Google Places and Trustpilot, cached temporarily to serve your widgets quickly.
  • Usage data: anonymous, aggregated counts per account (e.g. widget views, blocked or redirected visits) used for plan limits and analytics. We do not build profiles of you or your visitors.
  • Billing data: payments are processed by Stripe; we receive your plan, status and country/tax info but never see or store your card details.
  • Support data: emails you send us and their content.

3. Your site visitors: Bouncer & Router

When you enable Bouncer or Router, each visit triggers a real-time, server-side check from the visitor's IP address, before your page is shown, to determine:

  • the visitor's approximate country (and, on higher tiers, region/city), using a local geolocation database with ip-api.com as a fallback lookup; and
  • whether the connection appears to be a VPN, proxy or datacenter IP.

We use the IP address only to make the allow/block (Bouncer) or redirect/suggest (Router) decision. We do not store visitors' IP addresses and do not build profiles of them. There is no automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects on an individual, the decision only determines which page a browser sees. Where a plan keeps analytics, they are aggregated and non-identifying (e.g. blocked/redirected visits by country), never individual IP addresses.

Router may store a small functional value in the visitor's own browser (e.g. to remember a dismissed suggestion banner or a chosen region so they are not redirected twice). This is strictly for functionality, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not shared with us.

You are the data controller for this visitor processing and Vanssay acts as your processor, carrying out the geolocation and VPN check on your behalf and on your documented instructions (your rules). You are responsible for providing any privacy notice and lawful basis your jurisdiction requires when filtering or redirecting visitors. A data-processing addendum is available at support@vanssay.net.

4. Maintenance Mode

Maintenance Mode compares a visitor's IP against the whitelist you configure, server-side, to decide whether they see the maintenance page or the live site. The IP is used only for that check and is not stored. As above, you are the controller and Vanssay is your processor for this processing.

5. How we use data

Exclusively to operate the Service: authenticating you, fetching and displaying your reviews, enforcing your Bouncer/Router/Maintenance rules, applying plan limits, processing subscriptions, and sending essential transactional emails (from no-reply@vanssay.net via Resend). We never sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. Data retention & deletion

Review caches expire automatically (short TTL). Visitor IP checks are transient and are not logged with IP addresses. Aggregated analytics and your account and configuration data are kept while your account is active. Billing records are kept as long as required by law (typically up to 10 years for accounting). To delete your account and all associated personal data, email support@vanssay.net; we process deletion requests within 30 days, except records we must retain by law.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Exercise any of these by emailing support@vanssay.net. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in France, the CNIL. If your data is processed by a Vanssay plugin on a site you visited, direct your request to that site's owner (the controller), who can instruct us as their processor.

9. Cookies

Our website and account pages use only strictly necessary cookies/local storage (e.g. your login session). We set no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. On your published site, our widgets and checks set no tracking cookies on your visitors; Router may set a single functional value as described in §3.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access tokens are signed and time-limited. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and the relevant authority of any breach as required by law.

11. Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the Service evolves; material changes are announced here with an updated date. Questions or requests: support@vanssay.net.
