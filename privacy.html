Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Vanssay ("we", "us") provides Framer plugins and related services. This currently includes Google & Trustpilot Reviews (which displays public reviews on your site) and Bouncer (which controls site access by visitor country and VPN/proxy). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Plugin configuration — your settings for each plugin: for Reviews, the Google Business Profile and/or Trustpilot page you connect plus your widget settings; for Bouncer, your blocking rules (countries, VPN/proxy options, protected pages) and screen styling.
  • Review data — publicly available reviews fetched from Google Places and Trustpilot, cached temporarily to serve your widgets quickly.
  • Usage data — anonymous, aggregated counts per account (e.g. widget views, or for Bouncer the number of blocked visits) used for plan limits and analytics. We do not profile your site's visitors; our plugins set no cookies on them.
  • Billing data — payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.

2. Bouncer: how we handle your site visitors

When you enable Bouncer, each visit triggers a real-time, server-side check from the visitor's IP address — before your page is shown — to determine:

  • the visitor's approximate country, using a local geolocation database with ip-api.com as a fallback lookup; and
  • whether the connection appears to be a VPN, proxy or datacenter IP.

We use the IP address only to make this allow/block decision. We do not store visitors' IP addresses and do not build profiles of them. On the Ultra plan we keep aggregated, non-identifying analytics (such as blocked visits by country and VPN attempts) — never individual IP addresses.

For your site's visitors, you are the data controller and Vanssay acts as your processor, carrying out the geolocation and VPN check on your behalf. You are responsible for providing any notice and lawful basis your jurisdiction requires when filtering visitors.

3. How we use it

Exclusively to operate the service: authenticating you, fetching and displaying your reviews, enforcing your Bouncer rules, applying plan limits, processing subscriptions, and sending essential transactional emails (e.g. password resets from no-reply@vanssay.net via Resend). We do not sell or rent personal data, ever.

4. Third-party processors

  • Google Places API — review retrieval (subject to Google's terms).
  • Trustpilot — review retrieval (subject to Trustpilot's terms).
  • ip-api.com — fallback IP geolocation lookup for Bouncer.
  • Stripe — payments and billing.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.

5. Data retention & deletion

Review caches expire automatically. Bouncer visitor checks are transient and are not logged with IP addresses; aggregated Bouncer analytics are kept while your account is active. Account and configuration data are kept while your account is active. To delete your account and all associated data, email support@vanssay.net — we process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. Contact us at support@vanssay.net to exercise any of these rights.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access tokens are signed and time-limited. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions? support@vanssay.net
