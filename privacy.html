Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Vanssay collects, why, on what legal basis, and your rights. It applies to our website (vanssay.net) and to our plugins and apps wherever they run, including the Framer plugins and the Webflow Apps (Designer Extensions and their published-site code): Google & Trustpilot Reviews, Instagram Feed, Bouncer, Router and Maintenance Mode. Not every plugin is available on every platform.

Data controller. Marin de Vanssay (sole trader), operating vanssay.net, is the controller of the account data described here. Contact for any privacy request: support@vanssay.net.
Two roles. For your account and billing, we are the controller. For anything we do on behalf of your published site — serving your Reviews or Instagram widget, or running a Bouncer, Router or Maintenance check — you are the controller and we act as your processor (see §§3–6).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). Optionally the plugin you signed up from.
  • Plugin configuration: your settings per plugin, for Reviews, the profiles/pages you connect plus widget settings; for Bouncer, your blocking rules and screen styling; for Router, your redirect/suggestion rules (countries, regions, languages, devices, VPN options, destinations, UTM settings); for Maintenance Mode, your whitelist, page content and schedule; for Instagram Feed, the public Instagram handle you connect plus your layout, display settings and, in Curated mode, which posts you selected.
  • Third-party content you connect: publicly available reviews fetched from Google Places and Trustpilot, and publicly available Instagram profile information and posts fetched via Apify. All of it is cached temporarily on our servers so your widgets load quickly (typically 24 hours for the posts a widget displays, up to 7 days for the larger set the Curated picker offers). For Instagram feeds you have added, we also keep copies of the images and videos themselves on our servers and serve them to your visitors from there (see §6).
  • Usage data: anonymous, aggregated counts per account (e.g. widget views, blocked or redirected visits) used for plan limits and analytics. In the plugin interface we also record which step you reached (opened, previewed, reached the paywall, subscribed) against a random identifier generated in your browser and stored alongside your session, so we can see where people get stuck. It is not linked to any advertising network, contains no IP address or device fingerprint, and clearing the plugin's site data removes it. We do not build profiles of you or your visitors.
  • Webflow authorization (Webflow Apps only): if you let the App add its script to your site automatically, we store the site identifier and an access token issued by Webflow, encrypted at rest on our servers and never exposed to your browser. It is used solely to register that one script and apply it to your site's footer, is scoped to reading your site list and managing custom code, and can be withdrawn at any time by uninstalling the App in Webflow or using Remove from this site in the App. We delete the token and the site identifier when access is withdrawn.
  • Billing data: payments are processed by Stripe; we receive your plan, status and country/tax info but never see or store your card details.
  • Support data: emails you send us and their content.

3. Reviews & your site visitors

Reviews fetches the publicly available reviews of the Google and Trustpilot profiles you connect (review text, rating, author display name and photo as published by the platform, and date). We retrieve these server-side through the Google Places API and Trustpilot and cache them on our servers, typically for 24 hours, so your widget loads quickly and we do not re-fetch on every page view. No API key or credential is ever placed in your published page, and your visitors' browsers never contact Google or Trustpilot to display your widget.

Requests your published page makes. Like Instagram Feed, and unlike Bouncer, Router and Maintenance Mode, Reviews performs no per-visitor check and no IP geolocation. The widget on your published site calls our API at api.vanssay.net only, for these purposes:

RequestWhyWhat is sent
/widget-dataLoad the current reviews to display, so your page shows live content instead of a snapshot frozen at insert timeYour account and widget identifiers, and your own display settings (which platforms, minimum rating)
/trackIncrement an aggregate view counter for your widget, used solely to apply your plan's monthly view limitYour account and widget identifiers, and the path of the page the widget is on
/track/statusTell the widget whether your plan is within its limit, so it knows what to renderYour account identifier
/track/clickCount clicks on the free-plan "Powered by Vanssay" link. Only sent if a visitor deliberately clicks that link, never automatically on page loadYour account identifier and the path of the page

The identifiers in these requests are yours, the site owner's, they identify your account and your widget so we can serve the right reviews and count against the right plan. They say nothing about the individual visitor. We do not set any cookie, do not write to localStorage or any other browser storage, do not fingerprint the device, and do not create a record of any individual visitor. Counts are aggregate totals only, we cannot tell from them who visited, how many distinct people visited, or what any one person did. As with all our plugins, we do not build profiles of you or your visitors, and none of this data is shared with any advertising network.

As with any web server, our API necessarily sees the IP address of a browser that calls it in order to send a response back. We use it only to serve the response and to apply anti-abuse rate limits; we do not store visitors' IP addresses against your widget and do not use them for analytics.

You are the data controller for your published site and Vanssay acts as your processor when serving your widget. A data-processing addendum is available at support@vanssay.net.

4. Your site visitors: Bouncer & Router

When you enable Bouncer or Router, each visit triggers a real-time, server-side check from the visitor's IP address, before your page is shown, to determine:

  • the visitor's approximate country (and, on higher tiers, region/city), using a local geolocation database with ip-api.com as a fallback lookup; and
  • whether the connection appears to be a VPN, proxy or datacenter IP.

We use the IP address only to make the allow/block (Bouncer) or redirect/suggest (Router) decision. We do not store visitors' IP addresses and do not build profiles of them. There is no automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects on an individual, the decision only determines which page a browser sees. Where a plan keeps analytics, they are aggregated and non-identifying (e.g. blocked/redirected visits by country), never individual IP addresses.

Router may store a small functional value in the visitor's own browser (e.g. to remember a dismissed suggestion banner or a chosen region so they are not redirected twice). This is strictly for functionality, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not shared with us.

You are the data controller for this visitor processing and Vanssay acts as your processor, carrying out the geolocation and VPN check on your behalf and on your documented instructions (your rules). You are responsible for providing any privacy notice and lawful basis your jurisdiction requires when filtering or redirecting visitors. A data-processing addendum is available at support@vanssay.net.

5. Maintenance Mode

Maintenance Mode compares a visitor's IP against the whitelist you configure, server-side, to decide whether they see the maintenance page or the live site. The IP is used only for that check and is not stored. As above, you are the controller and Vanssay is your processor for this processing.

6. Instagram Feed

Instagram Feed fetches the public profile information and posts of the Instagram account you connect (profile picture, name, biography, follower/post counts, and the posts themselves with their captions, like and comment counts). We retrieve this through Apify, a third-party scraping service, and cache it on our servers so your widget loads quickly and we do not re-fetch on every page view.

We do not ask for, receive or store your Instagram password, and the plugin does not log in to Instagram as you. You may only connect an account you own or are authorized to represent, and you are responsible for having the rights to display its content on your site.

Your site visitors. As with Reviews (see §3), and unlike Bouncer, Router and Maintenance Mode, Instagram Feed performs no per-visitor check and no IP geolocation. When a page containing your feed is viewed we increment an aggregate counter for that feed, used solely to apply your plan's monthly view limit. No visitor IP address, identifier or profile is stored, and no cookie is set on your visitors.

We store copies of your feed’s images and videos on our own servers. Instagram’s image links are signed and stop working after a few days, so a feed that pointed at them would go blank. When you insert a feed, sync it, or build a Curated set, we download the pictures and videos of the posts that feed displays and keep them on our servers, then serve them to your visitors from there. Your visitors’ browsers never contact Instagram to display your feed.

We store this media only for feeds you have actually added. Simply previewing a profile in the plugin downloads nothing: previews are passed through and kept only in the short-lived cache described above. When you delete a feed, the stored media for that Instagram account is deleted from our servers too, unless another account still has a feed connected to the same profile.

7. How we use data

Exclusively to operate the Service: authenticating you, fetching and displaying your reviews and Instagram posts, enforcing your Bouncer/Router/Maintenance rules, applying plan limits, processing subscriptions, and sending essential transactional emails (from no-reply@vanssay.net via Brevo). We never sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

9. Data retention & deletion

Review caches expire automatically (typically 24 hours). Widget view and click counts are kept only as aggregate running totals per account and billing period, never as individual visit records. Instagram media stored for a feed (see §6) is kept for as long as that feed exists and is deleted when you delete the feed, unless another account still connects the same Instagram profile. Visitor IP checks are transient and are not logged with IP addresses. Aggregated analytics and your account and configuration data are kept while your account is active. Billing records are kept as long as required by law (typically up to 10 years for accounting). To delete your account and all associated personal data, email support@vanssay.net; we process deletion requests within 30 days, except records we must retain by law.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Exercise any of these by emailing support@vanssay.net. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in France, the CNIL. If your data is processed by a Vanssay plugin on a site you visited, direct your request to that site's owner (the controller), who can instruct us as their processor.

11. Cookies

Our website and account pages use only strictly necessary cookies/local storage (e.g. your login session). We set no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. On your published site, our widgets and checks set no tracking cookies on your visitors. The Reviews and Instagram Feed widgets set no cookie and no browser storage of any kind on your visitors (see §3 and §6); Router may set a single functional value as described in §4.

12. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access tokens are signed and time-limited. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and the relevant authority of any breach as required by law.

13. Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the Service evolves; material changes are announced here with an updated date. Questions or requests: support@vanssay.net.
