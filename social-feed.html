Show the Instagram posts of an account you own or manage on your Webflow site. Pick one of seven layouts, place it from the Designer, publish. No Instagram login and no code to paste.

How it works

The posts are retrieved and stored on our servers, so your page only talks to one place and Instagram's expiring image links never break it.

Connect

Type the handle of an account you own or are authorized to represent, and preview it.

Choose a layout

Flip through all seven on your own posts before placing anything.

Insert

Authorize the App once per site. A labelled placeholder goes on your canvas.

Publish

The feed shows on your published site, in place of the placeholder.

Seven layouts

Each one is built for posts, with a popup that shows the caption, date and video playback.

ProfileGridSliderApertureCarouselMarqueeRainbow

What it adds to your site

One script, through Webflow's own Custom Code API. Nothing else.

Where

Your site's footer custom code, added only after you authorize the App and insert a feed. Your other custom code is left as it is.

What it loads

A fixed version of our script, with an integrity hash, plus the feed's posts and media. All from api.vanssay.net.

What it records

One view per page load for the feed's monthly count, and the site address. No cookies, no browser storage, nothing about the visitor.

How to remove it

Remove from this site in the App takes the script out. Then delete the placeholders and publish. Step by step.
