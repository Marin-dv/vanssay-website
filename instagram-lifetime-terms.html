1. What "Lifetime" means

"Lifetime" means the operating life of the Instagram Feed service, not your own lifetime and not an unlimited period of time. It is a licence to use the plugin and its backend for as long as we operate that service, subject to the guaranteed minimum in section 3.

It is not a perpetual right, not a transfer of ownership, and not a promise that the service will exist forever. Software that depends on other companies' platforms cannot honestly promise that, and we would rather say so here than imply otherwise.

2. What is included

A Lifetime purchase includes the Instagram Feed plugin at the tier described on the plan screen at the time you bought it. As of the current version that is:

  • 2 Instagram connections (feeds), with more available as a separate one-time purchase (see section 6)
  • Up to 12 posts displayed per feed
  • Weekly automatic refresh, plus 1 manual refresh per day
  • Up to 50,000 feed views per month
  • The curated picker, insertion into the Framer canvas, use on your own domains, and priority support

It also includes any new layouts and improvements we add to this plugin, at no extra cost.

It does not include:

  • Our other plugins and products, current or future, including any successor product sold under a different name
  • Features that require a materially higher ongoing cost to run, which we may offer as paid add-ons
  • Framer's own subscription, which you buy from Framer
  • Custom development, migration work, or support beyond ordinary product support

3. How long it is guaranteed

We commit to operating the Instagram Feed service for at least 10 years from the date of your purchase. For a purchase made in 2026, that means until at least 2036.

During that period we will keep the service running, keep your feeds syncing, and keep your widgets served, subject to the rest of this page.

After the guaranteed period ends, we may discontinue the service. If we do:

  • We will give you at least six months' written notice, sent to the email address on your Vanssay account. It is your responsibility to keep that address current and able to receive our email.
  • The service will keep working normally throughout the notice period, so you have time to replace it on any live site.
  • At the end of the notice period the feeds stop being served. Widgets already placed on your pages will simply stop showing content. Your Framer site is not otherwise affected.
  • No refund is due at that point, because the guaranteed period has been delivered in full.

If we discontinue the service before the guaranteed period ends for any reason other than those in section 4 or section 11, you may ask for a refund of the purchase price, reduced in proportion to the time already served.

You accept that:

  • Individual posts, images or videos may become unavailable.
  • Our data provider may change its pricing, its terms, or its ability to serve this data, and we may have to switch provider, change how often feeds refresh, or reduce what a feed can retrieve.

In that case we will give you as much notice as the circumstances allow, and we will tell you honestly what happened.

A Lifetime purchase is for normal use of the plugin on sites you own or manage for clients. It is not a licence to run the service on behalf of unrelated third parties, to build a competing product on top of it, or to make automated requests beyond what the plugin itself makes.

6. Extra feeds

Additional Instagram connections beyond those included are available as a separate one-time purchase, at the price shown in the plugin at the time you buy them. Each extra feed is covered by these same conditions, including the guaranteed period in section 3, measured from your original Lifetime purchase date.

The price of an extra feed can change. Your Lifetime purchase fixes the price of what it included, and nothing more. It does not entitle you to buy additional feeds at the price that applied when you bought, and it does not oblige us to keep offering them at all. Every extra feed is charged at the price published at the moment you add it.

The reason is straightforward: each additional feed is an ongoing cost to us for as long as it runs, paid to Instagram data providers whose own prices we do not control. Extra feeds you have already bought are unaffected by any later change; you are never charged again for them.

10. Your content and our copies

The Instagram content displayed by your feed belongs to its authors and to Instagram. You confirm you own or represent the account you connect and that you have the right to display its posts, captions and images on your site.

To serve your feed reliably we store the handle, your display settings, and cached copies of posts and images on our servers. Instagram's image links expire quickly, so keeping copies is what stops your site showing broken images. A widget also carries a copy of the posts that were present when you inserted it, which means a feed can briefly show content after it is deleted on Instagram.

If you need content removed from our cache, write to support@vanssay.net and we will remove it. Deleting a feed stops further updates and removes it from our systems, but it does not alter pages you have already published; you also need to remove the widget in Framer and republish.
