FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else: drop me a line. It's one person, and he answers.

Is the demo on this page the real widget?

Yes, literally. The stylesheet this page loads is the plugin's own widget CSS, copied out of the source with no edits, and the markup is the same DOM the published component renders. If a card looks like that here, it looks like that on your site.

Whose reviews are in the demo?

A sample café — a fictional business with a realistic spread of five-, four- and two-star reviews, so the layouts are shown handling the awkward ones too. Inside the plugin you search your own business and every preview switches to your real reviews before anything is inserted.

Do I need to write any code?

No. It's a Framer plugin: connect your business, pick a layout, click insert. What lands on the canvas is a real Framer code component — you can move it, resize it and restyle it from Framer's right-hand panel like any other layer.

How does the theme matching work?

Before inserting, the plugin reads your project's text styles, colour styles, corner radii and the background colour where the widget will land, then bakes those tokens into the component. It looks native from the first second, and every token stays editable afterwards.

How fresh are the reviews?

They come straight from your Google Business Profile and your Trustpilot page. On Starter, Pro and Agency the widget re-fetches on every page load. On Free you refresh from the plugin; on Lifetime it re-syncs weekly.

Why does Google cap at 20 reviews?

Because that's what the provider actually returns per fetch, and quoting a number we can't deliver would just mean your preview and your live site show different reviews. Twenty per platform, forty combined, is the real ceiling — so that's what's on the pricing table.

Does one subscription cover several projects?

Yes. The plan lives on your account, not on a project. Pro connects up to five businesses and Agency up to thirty, each with its own reviews, layout and card design.

What happens if I hit my monthly view cap?

Nothing breaks and no “upgrade” box appears to your visitors. The widget quietly shows three reviews instead of your full set until the counter resets next month, and you get an email the moment it happens.
