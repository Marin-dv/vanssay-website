FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are these the real widgets?

Yes — the previews above use the same cards, layouts and animations your visitors will see. On your site they'll show your real Google & Trustpilot reviews, matched to your fonts and colors.

Do I need to write any code?

No. Vanssay is a Framer plugin: connect your reviews, click insert, and the widget appears on your canvas, already styled to match your site.

How does theme matching work?

Before inserting, Vanssay reads your project's text styles, color styles, corner radii and the background where the widget will land — then bakes those tokens in so it looks native from the first second.

Are the reviews up to date?

Reviews come straight from your Google Business Profile and Trustpilot page. On paid plans, widgets re-sync automatically on every page load.

Does one subscription cover multiple projects?

Yes — your plan is tied to your account, not a project. Use Vanssay on every Framer site you build.
