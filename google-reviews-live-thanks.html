Connect your Google reviews

The component reads your reviews directly from Google, so it needs your own Google API key and your Place ID. That takes about five minutes in Google Cloud Console, and the guide walks through every step including the key restrictions that keep it safe to publish.

Open the setup guide

One thing worth doing properly. Restrict your Google API key to your own domain before you publish. The key is visible in the page source of any site that uses it, and an unrestricted key can be used by anyone. Step 3 of the guide covers it.

If something is not working

The component explains problems on the Framer canvas, and the guide has a troubleshooting table matching every message to its fix. If you are still stuck, email support@vanssay.net and include what the canvas says.
